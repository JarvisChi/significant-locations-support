Notification History Archive — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Notification History Archive stores your notification archive on your device. The app does not require an account and has no advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs, or developer server receiving your notifications.

Information stored on your device

The app saves only information passed to its Save Notification action by a shortcut you configure. This can include notification text, titles, dates, app names and icons, sender or recipient details, and associated content. The app also stores your preferences and whether notifications are read or saved.

This information stays in the app’s local storage. The app cannot read Notification Center, list all your installed apps, or retrieve notifications from before setup. Your device backups may include the archive, depending on your Apple backup settings.

Shortcuts and test notifications

You choose the apps that the Shortcuts automation captures and can disable the automation at any time. Setup can send a local test notification, with your permission, to check that the shortcut saves it. Opening the shared shortcut uses Apple’s iCloud and Shortcuts services.

Subscriptions

Apple processes subscription purchases, payments, and restores. The app uses StoreKit to check whether Full History access is active. We do not receive your payment details, and notification content is never included in the subscription check.

Sharing and external links

Copying, sharing, or exporting notifications sends the information to the destination you select. Opening a link saved in a notification opens the relevant website or app, whose privacy practices apply. These actions are initiated by you.

Your choices

You can delete individual notifications or the entire archive in the app, choose an automatic-deletion period, or disable capture in Shortcuts. Saved notifications are protected from automatic deletion. Deleting an archived copy does not change the original app’s notification or copies you have exported. Device backups are managed through your Apple settings.

Support
