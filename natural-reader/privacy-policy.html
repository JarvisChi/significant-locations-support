Natural Reader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Your readings, imported audio, transcripts, playback positions, preferences, and prepared audio are stored in this app’s private storage on your device. Device backups may include these files according to your Apple backup settings. Deleting a reading removes its saved audio and word timings from this installation. Copies you shared or backed up must be deleted separately.

The app does not require an account, include advertising or developer analytics, track you across apps, or send your library or API key to a developer server. Apple and Microsoft services have their own privacy practices.

Siri and transcription

Siri narration passes the text you choose to Apple’s Shortcuts app. Review a shortcut’s actions before installing it. Apple provides the voice and may require voice or speech-model downloads. Imported audio transcription and word timing are requested on device. On older systems the app asks for Speech Recognition permission and requires on-device recognition; it does not fall back to sending recordings to Apple’s speech servers. If local transcription is unavailable, import a .naturalreading file prepared on another device.

Optional Azure — advanced users only

Azure is optional and requires your own endpoint and API key. When you select Azure and generate narration, the reading text is sent to your Azure Speech resource. Microsoft receives the text and connection information needed to provide its service. Microsoft’s terms and privacy policy apply, and your account may be billed. Audio already prepared can play offline.

Your Azure credentials are stored in this device’s Keychain, are not synchronized through iCloud Keychain, and are not included in exported readings. Remove them using the reading’s voice picker → Azure connection → Remove Azure connection. Your key is never prefilled into the visible key field. The local character count is an estimate of submitted text, not a billing record.

Optional tips

Apple processes optional in-app tips through the App Store. The app receives transaction information to verify and acknowledge the purchase; it does not receive your payment card details or send transaction information to a developer server. All features are already available in the free app. Tips do not unlock anything.

Sharing

Export Audio shares an audio file. Share Reading with Highlighting shares the selected title, full text, audio, and word timings. It does not include your Azure connection, other readings, favorites, or listening position. Anyone who receives a shared file can access its contents. Only share content you intend to disclose.

Support and this website

If you email significantlocations@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide so we can respond. Send only details needed to describe the issue; never send API keys or private readings. You can ask us to delete support correspondence, subject to any legal retention requirements.

The public support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as your IP address, when you visit. These pages contain no advertising or analytics scripts.
