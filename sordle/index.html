Sordle helps you crack five-letter word puzzles fast. Enter your letters, tap tiles to mark them as correct or misplaced, and Sordle instantly narrows the possibilities. Behind the scenes, the app ranks words using entropy or frequency scoring to show the most promising guesses first.
