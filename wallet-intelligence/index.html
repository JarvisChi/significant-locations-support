Wallet Intelligence

Track expenses, organize receipts, and understand spending by category, currency, or trip.

Capture and review purchases

Open Settings → Notifications & Automations to install a prepared shortcut. Choose the bank apps, message senders, or Wallet cards you want to capture, and enable its automation in Shortcuts. Capture starts after setup; it cannot recover past notifications.

Review uncertain details in Alerts. Receipt extraction can make mistakes, so check items and totals before saving.

Apple Intelligence

Settings → Apple Intelligence lets you choose Local or Cloud. Local needs a compatible device with Apple Intelligence enabled and its model ready. Cloud uses Apple’s Private Cloud Compute, needs an internet connection, and is subject to availability and usage limits. Manual entry remains available without AI.

FinanceKit

FinanceKit access depends on the app build, your device, region, eligible accounts, and permission. It is not a feed of every Apple Pay card or transaction. You choose the accounts and history you share through Apple’s permission interface.

Contact
