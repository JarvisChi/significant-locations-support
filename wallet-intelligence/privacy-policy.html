Wallet Intelligence — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Wallet Intelligence helps you track expenses, organize receipts and trips, and understand your spending. The app has no account system, advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs, or developer-operated server receiving your financial records. We do not sell your data.

Information you choose to add

The app stores expenses and notes you enter, receipts you photograph or select, trips you create, and information passed by Shortcuts automations you configure. Captured information may include notification or message text, merchant names, dates, amounts, currencies, card or pass descriptions, and source app names and icons. Original source details may be retained so you can review and correct an expense.

The app cannot browse Notification Center, read your Messages database, or recover alerts from before capture was enabled. Trip destinations and dates are entered by you; the app does not track your device’s location.

Optional FinanceKit access

Where FinanceKit is available and enabled, you may grant access to eligible accounts in Apple Wallet. Apple’s permission interface lets you choose which accounts and available history to share. Shared information can include account names and identifiers, financial institution names, balances, and transaction details such as amounts, currencies, descriptions, merchant names and category codes, dates, and transaction status. FinanceKit does not give us your bank sign-in credentials or permission to move money.

FinanceKit information is used only to provide the financial management functions you request, such as maintaining transaction history, categorizing expenses, reconciling duplicate captures, and showing spending summaries. It is not used for advertising, marketing, tracking, or app personalization, and is not sold or shared with data brokers. When ongoing access is enabled, the app may receive updates in the background.

You can change or revoke FinanceKit access in your device’s privacy settings. Stopping access prevents new retrieval; it does not automatically erase copies you previously saved or exported. You can remove saved information using the app’s deletion controls.

Apple Intelligence: Local or Cloud

Local processing is the default. On a supported device, Apple Intelligence processes the input for an AI feature on your device. Receipt text recognition, transaction filtering, totals, and bill-splitting arithmetic run locally.

If you select Cloud in Settings → Apple Intelligence, input needed for an AI request is sent to Apple’s Private Cloud Compute. Depending on the feature, this can include captured alert text, receipt images and recognized text, your question, and relevant trip or conversation context. It may contain financial or personal information. We do not route these requests through a developer-operated server or another AI provider. Apple describes its Private Cloud Compute protections on its Private Cloud Compute page. You can switch back to Local at any time. This setting is separate from iCloud sync.

Storage, iCloud, and retention

Saved records and preferences are stored on your device. If you enable Sync with My iCloud, the app’s saved expenses, trips, alerts, and receipts may also be stored in your private iCloud database. Device backups follow your Apple settings. The developer has no access to your private iCloud database.

Saved records remain until you delete them. Ask Wallet conversation history is held for the current app session. Deleting an expense keeps its original alert and receipt available for separate review or deletion; deleting a receipt does not delete its expense. Copies you exported or included in device backups are managed separately. Turning off sync does not erase existing copies in iCloud.

Shortcuts and notifications

You control which apps, messages, and Wallet cards your Shortcuts automations capture. Disable those automations in Shortcuts to stop future capture. With your notification permission, the app can send setup tests, transaction confirmations, and review reminders. Notifications may display transaction information according to your device’s notification preview settings. Installing a shared shortcut uses Apple’s Shortcuts and iCloud services.

Purchases, exports, and support

Apple processes App Store purchases and restores. The app uses StoreKit to check access; we do not receive your payment card details, and your financial records are not included in that check.

Sharing or exporting sends the information you select to your chosen destination. Its privacy practices then apply. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you send so we can respond. Please avoid including full account numbers or other unnecessary sensitive information.

This website and policy updates
